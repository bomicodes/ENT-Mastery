How it works

  • Read the front and answer in your head before you flip. Saying the answer to yourself is what makes it stick; recognizing it on the back does not.
  • Rate honestly. Again: you missed it, and it comes back in a minute. Hard: you got it with effort. Good: you knew it. Easy: instant, so push it out further. Each button shows when you'll see the card next.
  • Everything you answer elsewhere counts. A question you miss in the bank or a timed exam becomes a due card right away; one you get right is scheduled for later.
  • Do your reviews daily. Due cards pile up if you skip days, while new cards are capped, so on a busy day do the reviews and skip new ones.
  • Keyboard: space or Enter flips the card; 1, 2, 3 and 4 rate it.
