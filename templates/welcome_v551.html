Your first 15 minutes

  1. Get a baseline. Build a 20-question timed block with "Board blueprint mix" checked. The score report shows your weak domains.
  2. Open your Daily Path. It builds a short session from your weak areas and anything due for review. This is the one page to open every day.
  3. Look up your next case. Search a procedure in OR Tomorrow for the steps, landmarks, danger structures and postoperative watch-points before you scrub.

What's here

Daily PathA personalized 10-30 minute session every day: review, weak spots and new material. Clinical Challenges{{ '{:,}'.format(stats.board) }} curated board-style vignettes with explanations, why each wrong answer is wrong, and a curveball follow-up. Concept ChecksFast recall prompts for core facts. Use them before vignettes on a new topic. Timed ExamBoard-length blocks in timed or tutor mode, weighted to the blueprint, from unused, missed, flagged or image questions. Interpretation AtlasReal images and tracings: endoscopy, CT/MRI, pathology, ultrasound, audiograms, VNG, sleep studies and more. Anatomy AtlasSurgical maps with landmarks, danger structures and linked anatomy cases. OR TomorrowProcedure cards for the night before: indications, key steps, landmarks to identify, what can hurt the patient, and the early and late postoperative course. Mock Oral BoardPractice the oral exam out loud: commit, defend, handle complications. H&N StagingAJCC 8 TNM tables by site, memory hooks and practice cases. Deep CurriculumReading for each topic, organized by domain, with sources.

How it keeps track

  • Mastery per topic. Every answer updates your mastery for that concept. See it in My Progress.
  • Missed questions come back. A miss is due again in 1 day, then 3, then 7. It leaves the queue after two correct answers in a row. Due items appear on the dashboard and in the Daily Path.
  • Your data is yours. Each resident signs in with their own name and PIN. Change your PIN under Change PIN, or start over with Reset progress.

A suggested week before boards

Every dayDaily Path, 20-30 minutes.
Twice a weekA 40-question timed block on the blueprint mix, then review every miss.
Once a weekOne Interpretation Atlas lab and one Mock Oral Board case.
Before each OR dayThe OR Tomorrow card for your case.

Good to know

  • On your phone or offline. Use "Install ENT Mastery" in the menu to add it to your home screen. Offline Questions keeps a 400-question set you can practice without signal; answers sync when you reconnect.
  • Spot an error? Every question has a "Report a problem" link. Reports go to the chief review queue and get fixed or pulled.
  • Teaching juniors? Each question has a board pearl and a curveball you can use as a teaching prompt, and the Mock Oral Board works well as a group exercise.
